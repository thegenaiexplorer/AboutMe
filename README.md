# AboutMe
I am:
- Driven by curiosity.
- A few-shot learner.
- A multipotentialite / polymath.
Rest everything follows...

My career has been less about staying within one technology or role and more about understanding how things work, how they connect, and how they can be made better.

Not long ago, I was managing and patching Solaris/Linux clusters and architecting zero-downtime recovery systems for JP Morgan Chase and Cisco Systems — institutions where "downtime" isn't a metric, it's a headline - downtimes get 'heads' rolling 😂.

Somewhere between debugging SCSI timeout issues with Red Hat's engineering team and orchestrating disaster recovery events across time zones, I got restless. Hung up and cleared the CFA(L1/L2), built (and eventually wound down) an EdTech venture, and decided the next problem worth obsessing over was AI.

Today, my work centers on Deep Learning, Generative AI, AI Systems and Model Optimization—from experimenting with architectures and training models to optimizing inference and engineering for real-world deployment constraints.

My hands-on work spans LLMs, Computer Vision, NLP, synthetic data, anomaly detection, RAG, PEFT/alignment, MLOps and inference optimization. I have worked with quantization/QAT, pruning, knowledge distillation, KV caching, speculative decoding, LoRA/QLoRA, DPO/GRPO, distributed inference and hardware-aware optimization.

My Generative AI journey began well before the current LLM wave. During my M.S. research, I explored WGAN-GP and VAEs for synthetic tabular data generation, AE/VAE-based anomaly detection, and designed a hybrid classification–anomaly detection framework for highly imbalanced fraud detection using Deep Learning/Generative AI techniques.

Before AI became my primary focus, I spent 15+ years in enterprise technology, progressing through Systems Administration, Technical Leadership, Architecture and Technology Project Management, with deep experience in UNIX/Linux, storage, HA clusters, disaster recovery, migrations and critical production environments.

What ties everything together is the bias toward understanding a system deeply enough to rebuild it from scratch, then making it fast, cheap, and production ready.
The thread connecting it all is simple: I enjoy learning difficult things, connecting ideas across disciplines, experimenting, and building.

My interests include AI Systems, Applied AI, Generative AI, Computer Vision, LLM Systems, Model Optimization and Forward-Deployed AI Engineering—where deep technical exploration meets real-world engineering.  

If our interests overlap, let us talk!!
